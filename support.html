Support

Answers to the usual questions, and how to reach us when they don’t help.

Write to us

chandler@sapphireproductionsllc.com

Say what happened and which phone you play on (the model and its iOS or Android version). For a purchase, include the order number from the store’s receipt email: an Apple order ID, or a Google Play order number starting with GPA.

Purchases

I bought something and don’t have it.

Open the game with an internet connection: purchases that didn’t finish are delivered when it starts. If it’s still missing, go to Settings (the gear on the Ramp) → Restore purchases. Still nothing? Write to us with the order number.

How do I get my purchases on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account, install the game and tap Settings → Restore purchases. Permanent purchases, such as the Owner’s pack and the Manager pack, come back. Time warps and airport packs pay out once, so they don’t.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you paid. On an iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, follow Google Play’s refund steps.

Your game

Where is my game saved?

On your phone only; the game has no accounts. A new phone starts a new game unless you restore it from a backup of your old phone (iCloud or Google’s backup). Your purchases come back either way; see above.

How does time away work?

While you’re away, your managers keep the ramp running on the spots they cover, and the game pays you for it when you come back. The Away chip on the Ramp shows what you earn a minute and for how many hours. Bigger airports and more time played lengthen it, and the Owner’s pack makes it 24 hours.

Can I start over?

Settings → Reset progress starts again at Skyhaven Field, Legacy and all. Your store purchases stay. It can’t be undone.

Ads, sound and privacy

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads only ever play when you choose one for a reward, such as a boost or doubled earnings from time away. The Owner’s pack gives every boost without an ad.

How do I turn the sound or music off?

Settings has switches for Sound effects, the Ramp sound and Music, and volume sliders for the ramp sound and music. On an iPhone, the game is also silent while the phone is in silent mode.

How do I change my ad and privacy choices?

Where the game asked for your consent to ads, Settings → Privacy choices lets you change your answer. The Privacy Policy explains what the game collects and the rest of your choices.
